# Linkest Foundation

> **Link this galaxy, with your content.**

**Linkest Foundation** is an entity-less, non-profit open-source foundation dedicated to building secure, zero-trust infrastructure for independent creators and open-source projects.

We believe that creators should own their data, their code, and their future. Our mission is to provide the legal, technical, and infrastructural scaffolding that allows independent developers to focus on what they do best: creating.

---

## 🏛️ What We Do

### 🛡️ Security & CNA Governance
We operate a rigorous vulnerability disclosure ecosystem. As a participant in the **CVE Program** (via the MITRE Automation Working Group and Outreach & Communications Working Group), we advocate for a security-first approach.
*   **Strict Disclosure**: We follow a 90-day responsible disclosure timeline.
*   **CVE Assignment**: Vulnerabilities affecting our affiliated projects are coordinated and assigned CVE IDs through our centralized GHSA process.
*   **Hardware-Backed Security**: We encourage and support the use of FIDO2/Passkeys (e.g., YubiKey) for all critical infrastructure access.

### 🚀 Project Incubation & Hosting
We provide a safe harbor for independent projects. We offer:
*   **GitHub Organization Hosting**: A centralized, audited space for code collaboration.
*   **Edge Infrastructure**: Zero-cost, highly available serverless deployment via Cloudflare Workers, Pages, and D1.
*   **Status Monitoring**: Mandatory onboarding to our public status page (`status.linkest.org`) for transparency and reliability.

### 📜 Legal & Compliance
We protect creators' rights through strict open-source compliance:
*   **Dual Licensing**: Code is typically licensed under **GPL-3.0** or **AGPL-3.0**. Creative assets (art, music, lore) are licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.
*   **Contributor License Agreement (CLA)**: All contributors must sign our CLA to ensure the foundation can legally protect and sustain the ecosystem.

## 📥 Join the Ecosystem

We are looking for passionate independent developers, designers, and security researchers. Whether you have a finished project or just a roadmap, you are welcome here.

**Admission Requirements:**
1.  **A Project or Roadmap**: You must have an active open-source project or a detailed development plan.
2.  **GitHub Presence**: An active GitHub account with a public repository demonstrating real code history.
3.  **Transparency**: If your project has a public website, it must be onboarded to **Linkest Foundation Status Monitoring**.
4.  **Security First**: You must agree to our [Security Policy](https://linkest.org/security.html) and follow responsible disclosure principles.
5.  **AI Accountability**: AI tools are welcome, but human oversight is mandatory. You are solely responsible for any vulnerabilities introduced by AI-generated code.

**Read the full [Joining Guide & Code of Conduct](https://linkest.org/join.html) before applying.**
